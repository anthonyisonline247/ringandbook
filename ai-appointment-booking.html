Questions

AI appointment-booking FAQ

Does Ring & Booked replace Cal.com?

No. Ring & Booked can be configured to book into your Cal.com calendar. Your business remains in control of the calendar availability and appointment rules.

Can I decide what caller details are collected?

Yes. The booking flow is configured around the information your business needs for the types of appointments it allows.

What happens if a caller cannot be booked?

The receptionist can follow the human follow-up and escalation rules that you approve during setup instead of making an unapproved booking.
