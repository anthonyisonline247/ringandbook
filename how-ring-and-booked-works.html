How Ring & Booked works

A receptionist built around your rules—not a tool you configure alone.

Ring & Booked is a done-for-you AI phone receptionist for service businesses. You provide the operating details and approvals; we configure and test the call flow before the service goes live.

The setup process

Four practical stages before your callers use the service.

Share your operating details

Tell us about your services, business hours, service area, appointment rules, escalation contacts, and caller-language preferences.

Approve the call-handling rules

Decide what the receptionist may explain, which callers can be offered an appointment, and when a request should be handed to your team.

Configure and test

We configure the receptionist around the details you approve and test the experience with you before activation.

Launch and keep details current

Once the service is ready, you coordinate phone routing and keep us informed when operational details need to change.

What is configured

The parts of a caller experience that should reflect your business.

Call handling

Approved business information, services, caller details, and the follow-up paths your team wants to use.

Appointment booking

Eligible appointment types and Cal.com calendar availability, set around the booking rules you approve.

Human escalation

Transfer contacts and handoff instructions for situations where your business wants a person involved.

Clear boundaries build trust

What Ring & Booked does not do.

A configured receptionist can follow the business rules you approve. It should not replace your judgment, promise services outside those rules, or become your emergency procedure.

  • Ring & Booked is not a 911, utility-dispatch, or emergency-response service.
  • Immediate emergencies should be directed to 911, the applicable utility, or local emergency services.
  • Your business remains responsible for keeping hours, availability, contacts, and operating instructions current.
Billing and account clarity

Starter includes 250 minutes for $89 per month; Growth includes 700 minutes for $189 per month. Additional minutes are $0.35 per minute. Current plan and cancellation information are available at checkout and in your account.

Questions

How Ring & Booked works FAQ

Do I have to build the AI receptionist myself?

No. You provide approved business information and operating rules; Ring & Booked configures and tests the receptionist with you.

Can the receptionist use multiple languages?

Yes. Tell us which caller languages matter to your business. The enabled languages are configured and tested before launch.

How do I request a change after going live?

Keep your operating information current and contact the Ring & Booked team to arrange configuration changes.
