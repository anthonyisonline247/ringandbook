Onboarding checklist

Give your AI receptionist the business context it needs.

The best setup starts with the operating details your callers experience every day: services, hours, availability, human contacts, and the situations where your team wants to stay involved.

See how setup worksStart free setup

Ring & Booked configures and tests the call flow with you; it is not a self-serve agent builder.

Prepare these details

Bring the real operating rules, not just a business name.

Business profile

Confirm your business name, main contact, current customer-facing phone number, service area, and business hours.

Services and answers

List the services you want callers to hear about, the common questions you want handled, and the topics that should be handed to a person.

Appointment rules

Identify the appointment types that can be booked, the caller details required before booking, and the Cal.com availability you approve.

Escalation contacts

Provide current contacts, routing order, and the conditions under which a call should be transferred or flagged for follow-up.

Caller languages

Tell us which caller languages matter to your customers. Ring & Booked configures and tests the languages you choose before launch.

Emergency boundary

Keep your own immediate-emergency and utility procedures available. The receptionist should direct those callers to 911, the applicable utility, or local emergency services.

Before activation

Review the experience from the caller's point of view.

A short review can catch unclear services, outdated hours, missing contact numbers, or booking rules that are not ready for callers. Treat this as an operating review, not just a technical handoff.

  • Check your name, service descriptions, hours, and contact details.
  • Confirm what appointments can be offered and when.
  • Test calls in the languages and escalation scenarios you approve.
What Ring & Booked does

We turn the information you approve into a configured receptionist, test it with you, and coordinate launch after the service is ready. Read the full setup process.

Questions

AI receptionist onboarding FAQ

Can I update my business information later?

Business details change. Keep a designated contact who can request configuration changes when hours, services, availability, or escalation contacts change.

Can the receptionist be configured for more than one language?

Yes. Tell us the languages your callers need; the enabled languages are configured and tested with your business before launch.

When does the trial begin?

The seven-day trial begins when your configured phone service goes live, not when you create an account or start onboarding.
