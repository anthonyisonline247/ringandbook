A practical boundary

Urgent routing is configured—not assumed.

Your business defines the contacts and instructions it wants used. Ring & Booked is not an emergency-response service and should not replace your own emergency procedures or backup contact paths.

  • Use your approved contact numbers and transfer sequence.
  • Configure clear information collection before a follow-up.
  • Tell callers to contact 911, the applicable utility, or local emergency services for an immediate emergency.
