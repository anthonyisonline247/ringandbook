Questions

About this AI receptionist demo

Is this a self-serve AI agent builder?

No. Ring & Booked is delivered as a done-for-you service. You provide approved business information and operating rules; the team configures and tests the receptionist with you.

Can the receptionist book into a calendar?

When booking is configured for a business, Ring & Booked can write confirmed eligible appointments into its Cal.com calendar according to approved availability and appointment rules.

What happens with calls outside the approved flow?

The business chooses the handoff and human follow-up instructions for questions or callers that should not be answered or booked automatically.
