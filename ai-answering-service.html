Questions

AI answering service FAQ

Will the receptionist answer questions it was not configured to answer?

No. The call flow is based on your approved information and escalation rules. Questions outside that configuration can be sent to your team for follow-up.

Can callers book rather than leave a message?

Yes, when booking is configured for your service. Ring & Booked can write confirmed appointments into Cal.com according to the rules you approve.

How is the service priced?

Starter includes 250 minutes for $89 per month and Growth includes 700 minutes for $189 per month. Additional minutes are billed at the published $0.35 per minute rate.
