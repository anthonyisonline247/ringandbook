FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

It books for real. Ring & Booked integrates with Cal.com to write confirmed appointments directly into your calendar. You'll see the customer's name, phone, address, and service type — no callback required.
We configure the AI around your approved business information and escalation rules. Questions outside that configuration can be routed for human follow-up rather than answered automatically.
We work with you to define the caller greeting, the business information it can share, and any notices that fit your call flow. You retain control over how the receptionist represents your business and when it should hand a call to your team.
Ring & Booked supports 10+ languages including English, Spanish, Mandarin Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese, Japanese, French, Portuguese, Tagalog, and Hindi. Tell us which languages your customers speak, and our team configures and tests your AI in those languages before you go live — including automatic detection and mid-call switching for the ones you enable.
We configure urgent-call routing with your approved contacts and instructions before launch. Ring & Booked is not a 911, utility-dispatch, or emergency-response service. In an immediate emergency, callers should contact 911, the applicable utility, or local emergency services.
You fill out an onboarding form (about 20 minutes), we build and test your AI, and you're live within 1 business day. Setup is fully done-for-you — you just forward your existing phone number to us.
Recording, transcription, and AI summaries can be configured for your service where appropriate. We work with you on the caller notice and routing approach before enabling a recording-enabled call flow. Contact our team to request call information; call history is not yet available in your workspace.
Extra usage is billed at the published $0.35 per minute rate and appears on your invoice. Review your selected plan and billing terms at checkout.
New accounts get a 7-day free trial to put Ring & Booked on real calls before committing to a paid plan. We still do the full setup and testing for you first — the trial starts once your AI receptionist is live on your line.
