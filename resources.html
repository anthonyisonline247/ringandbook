What this does not replace

Keep your own operating and emergency procedures in place.

A receptionist can follow the rules you approve. It should not be asked to invent policies, replace your judgment, or act as an emergency-response service.

  • Maintain clear 911, utility, and local emergency instructions for immediate emergencies.
  • Keep a current internal list of contacts who may receive escalated calls.
  • Review booking availability and service boundaries before changing your phone routing.
